# Reveal trio — subset card only

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Status:** Approved  
**Completeness:** `docs/superpowers/reviews/completeness/2026-09-08-trio-subset-card-completeness.md`

## Why (trader’s call)

Default **I handle + no group** keeps qty/rate private (middle desk).  
**See each other On** is when the trader wants transparency with mill and buyer (or keeps deals verbal off-app). Not forced.

## Rules

1. Default path: **I handle + no group**.
2. Trio is opt-in (order mill card or Your paths for that mill × buyer).
3. **One group per mill** (trader + buyer + that supplier). Never one group with two mills.
4. Living card in the trio = **mill subset** (lot Sent to supplier), with **Part of #{main}**.
5. **Main** (buyer ↔ trader) living cards stay on the buyer↔trader 1:1 — do **not** post the main ticket into the trio.

## Multi-supplier

Order with mill A + mill B, both reveal On → two groups; each shows that mill’s subset only.
